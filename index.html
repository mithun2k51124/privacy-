Privacy Policy

GainQuest

✦ v1.0 · Effective Sep 30, 2026 📅 Last Updated: Sep 30, 2026 gainquest.myapp

GainQuest is built as a privacy-first, local-first fitness and workout tracking application. We strongly believe your personal health and fitness data belongs strictly to you.

🛡

Zero data transmission. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or transmit your personal data to external servers — ever.

1

Information We Do NOT Collect No accounts · No tracking · No ads

👤 No Account Required

You do not need to register with an email address, phone number, or social media account to use GainQuest. No sign-up, no sign-in.

☁ No Cloud Tracking or Sync

We do not operate proprietary backend servers that log your workout routines, personal records, body measurements, or progress photos.

📊 No Third-Party Analytics or Ad Networks

We do not embed behavioral advertising SDKs, data brokers, or user profiling trackers of any kind.

2

Information Handled Locally on Your Device SQLite · Sandboxed · 100% on-device

All your personal fitness activity is processed and stored exclusively on your device using local SQLite databases and local file storage:

  • Workout & Routine Logs: Sets, reps, weights, exercise templates, and completion history.
  • Body & Habit Metrics: Bodyweight logs, streak counters, and daily habit checklists.
  • Progress Photos: Photos you capture or pick stay exclusively in your device's local application sandbox storage.
✅

Your data never leaves your device. No uploads, no syncing, no remote processing — full ownership remains with you at all times.

3

Device Permissions & How They Are Used Minimal · Purpose-bound · Transparent

GainQuest only requests device permissions when strictly necessary to provide features you explicitly interact with:

📷 Camera CAMERA

Used solely for scanning food barcodes/nutrition labels and taking fitness progress photos. Camera feeds are analyzed in real time on the device and are never broadcasted or uploaded.

🎙 Audio & Microphone RECORD_AUDIO

Used only when you tap the voice workout logger to dictate exercise sets and reps. Speech processing is handled through on-device speech recognition APIs. Audio recordings are not saved or sent to remote servers.

🌐 Internet Access INTERNET

Used strictly for read-only external lookups: fetching publicly available nutrition and product information (such as OpenFoodFacts) when you scan a food barcode or search an ingredient. No personal identifiers or workout logs are transmitted during these requests.

4

Third-Party Services Public APIs only · No personal data shared

The App may query public APIs for food and nutrition information:

🥗 Public Nutrition Databases (e.g., OpenFoodFacts)

When searching for food or scanning a barcode, a query containing only the product barcode or food name is sent to retrieve public nutrition facts. No personal user data or identifiers are shared with these services.

ℹ

Queries to external APIs are anonymous and contain only the product identifier needed to fetch publicly available nutrition information. Your identity and health profile are never attached.

5

Data Retention & Deletion You are in full control

Because your data is stored 100% locally on your device:

  • You retain total ownership and control of your data at all times.
  • You can clear individual logs, exercises, or habits directly inside the App settings.
  • If you uninstall GainQuest or clear the app storage in your Android System Settings, all your local databases and progress files are permanently and irreversibly deleted.
⚠

Uninstalling the app or clearing app data will permanently remove all your workout history, logs, and photos. There is no cloud backup to restore from — export any data you wish to keep before doing so.

6

Children's Privacy Not directed to users under 13

GainQuest is not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since all data is stored locally without account creation, no personal data from minors is harvested.

If you believe a child under 13 has inadvertently used GainQuest and you have concerns, please contact us at mithun2k51124@gmail.com.

Continued use of GainQuest after changes to the Privacy Policy constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.
